Fox Escape – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Fox Escape ("the app") is a focus timer made by PANG WEI. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: we do not collect any personal data.

No account required

You can use Fox Escape without creating an account or signing in. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.

Data stays on your device

Your focus sessions, loot, Fox Den progress, outfits, streaks and weekly report are stored only on your iPhone. This data is not sent to us or to any third party. If you delete the app, this data is deleted too.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking SDKs. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Notifications and Live Activities

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local notifications on your device (for example, to remind you to return during an expedition). Live Activities show your current session on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Both are created on your device; no data is sent to a server.

Purchases and subscriptions

Fox Escape Plus subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is described in Apple's privacy policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
