常见问题

这个 App 是做什么的？

先设想十年后的自己（最多 3 个：事业、家庭、身体……），再从十年后一层一层倒推回今天：十年后 → 五年后 → 一年后 → 本月 → 本周 → 今天的一件小事。每完成一件，十年后的画像就清晰一点。

计划怎么往前走？

每周一打开 App 时会有每周回顾：标记上周哪些做到了、哪些太难，App 会推出新的一周；新的月份也会推出新的月目标。计划的每一步都可以自己修改。

如何订阅、取消订阅？

在 App 的“我的”页面可以解锁 Pro。年订阅含 7 天免费试用。订阅会自动续期，可随时在 iPhone 的“设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅”中取消；试用结束前取消不会收费。

换了手机，订阅不见了？

请用同一个 Apple 账户登录，然后在订阅页或“我的”页面点“恢复购买”。

我的资料会不会丢？

你的画像、计划、信件和回顾保存在手机上，并自动备份到你自己的 iCloud。换手机或重装 App 后，用同一个 Apple 账户登录即可恢复。

如何删除我的资料？

在“我的”页面点“删除全部数据”，会永久删除手机和 iCloud 里的所有内容。

AI 的内容可靠吗？

画像、计划和对话由 AI 生成，仅供参考，不是职业、医疗、法律或财务方面的专业建议。如果你正经历危机：美国或加拿大请拨打或短信 988，台湾请拨打 1925，香港请致电 2389 2222 或 2896 0000，紧急情况请拨打当地紧急电话。

FAQ

What does the app do?

You picture yourself ten years from now (up to 3 future selves: career, family, health…), then plan backward to today: 10 years → 5 years → 1 year → this month → this week → one small task today. Each task you finish makes your future portrait a little clearer.

How does the plan move forward?

Every Monday the app opens a weekly review: mark which steps you finished and which were too hard, and the app plans the next week (and the next month's goals at the start of a month). You can edit every step yourself.

How do I subscribe or cancel?

Unlock Pro from the Me tab. The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial. Subscriptions renew automatically; cancel any time in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Cancelling before the trial ends means you are not charged.

My subscription is missing on a new phone

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Restore Purchases on the paywall or in the Me tab.

Will I lose my data?

Your portraits, plans, letters and reflections are stored on your phone and backed up automatically to your own iCloud. On a new phone or after reinstalling, sign in with the same Apple Account to restore them.

How do I delete my data?

In the Me tab, tap Delete All Data. This permanently removes everything from your phone and iCloud.

Is the AI content reliable?

Portraits, plans and chat replies are generated by AI for reference only and are not professional career, medical, legal or financial advice.
