Clean Filter – Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 16, 2026

Clean Filter ("we", "our", or "us") is a skincare ingredient decoder app. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

Clean Filter does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All ingredient scanning and scoring happens entirely on your device.

Camera and Photo Library

The app requests access to your camera and photo library solely to read ingredient text from product packaging. Images are processed on-device and are never uploaded or stored.

Purchase Information

Subscription and purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple via the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Analytics

We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Clean Filter is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
