Fox Escape – Support

Need help with Fox Escape? Email gwayappleapp@outlook.com and we'll get back to you.

Why did my expedition fail?

An expedition fails if you tap Give Up, or if you leave the app for more than 30 seconds. Locking your iPhone is fine, as long as your iPhone has a passcode set.

How does the safe work?

During an expedition, tap any item you've found to lock it in your safe. If the run fails, items in the safe still come home. Free users have 1 safe slot; Fox Escape Plus gives you 3.

I don't see the Live Activity

Go to Settings › Fox Escape and make sure Live Activities are turned on.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open the Fox Escape Plus screen (tap Plus on the home screen) and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Fox Escape. You can cancel at any time; Plus stays active until the end of the current period.
