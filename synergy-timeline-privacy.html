Synergy Timeline – Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

Effective date: November 20, 2026 · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 20 日

Summary · 摘要

Synergy Timeline does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising. Synergy Timeline 不收集任何个人数据，没有账号、统计分析或广告。

What stays on your device · 保存在设备上的内容

The names and birth dates you enter, the moments you record, their notes and any photos you attach are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never sent to us or to any third party. 你输入的姓名和生日、记录的时刻、笔记以及添加的照片只保存在你的设备上，不会发送给我们或任何第三方。

Photos · 照片

If you choose to attach a photo, the app asks for access to your photo library and copies only the photo you pick into its own storage on the device. 只有在你选择添加照片时，App 才会请求访问照片图库，并只把你选中的照片复制到 App 在本机的存储中。

PDF export · 导出 PDF

When you export a PDF keepsake, the file is created on your device and shared only where you choose (for example AirDrop, Files or Mail). 导出的 PDF 在本机生成，只分享到你选择的位置。

Purchases · 购买

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. 订阅由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，我们不会获得你的付款信息。

Deleting your data · 删除数据

You can erase all data in Settings, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. 你可以在设置中删除全部数据，或删除 App 以清除所有内容。

Children · 儿童

Synergy Timeline is not directed at children under 13. 本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童。
