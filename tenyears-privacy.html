十年后的我 – 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 23 日

“十年后的我”是一款帮助你设想十年后的自己，再从那里倒推回今天的 App。本政策说明我们处理哪些信息、为什么处理，以及你可以怎样控制。

不需要账户

本 App 不要求注册或登录，不收集姓名、邮箱、电话等身份信息。

发送给 AI 的内容

当你生成画像、倒推计划，或和“十年后的我”对话时，你填写的描述（例如想成为什么样的人、现在的起点）和对话文字，会通过我们的服务器（托管于 Cloudflare Workers）转发给 Anthropic 的 Claude API，用来生成内容。我们的服务器只做转发，不保存、不记录这些内容。Anthropic 会按照其商业服务条款和隐私政策处理这些内容，可能会为安全和滥用监测保留一段有限的时间，默认不会用于训练模型。

请不要输入身份证号、银行卡号、密码等敏感信息。

保存在你设备和 iCloud 上的数据

你的画像、计划、每日任务、晚间回顾、给未来的信和对话记录保存在你的设备上，并通过 Apple 的 iCloud 键值存储同步到你自己的 iCloud 账户，方便换手机后恢复。我们无法读取你的 iCloud 内容。你可以随时在“我的 → 删除全部数据”中永久删除设备和 iCloud 上的这些数据。

匿名设备编号与限流

为防止滥用、控制服务成本，App 会生成一个随机的匿名设备编号，随请求发送给服务器，用于统计每天的 AI 使用次数。这个计数在服务器上最多保留 2 天。服务器也会临时使用你的 IP 地址做短时间的频率限制，不会单独保存。匿名设备编号和已使用的免费对话次数保存在设备的钥匙串中，删除 App 后仍会保留，用于防止重复领取免费次数；它们不包含任何个人信息。

购买与订阅

订阅通过 Apple App Store 处理。我们不会收到你的姓名、邮箱或付款信息，只会从 Apple 得知订阅是否有效。

通知

每晚的回顾提醒和信件到期提醒都是在你的设备上本地安排的通知，不经过我们的服务器。你可以在 App 或系统设置中关闭。

不做的事

本 App 不使用第三方统计或广告 SDK，不追踪你在其他 App 或网站上的行为，不出售任何数据。

儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的信息。

不是专业服务

AI 生成的画像、计划和回答仅供参考，不是职业、医疗、法律、财务或心理方面的专业建议。如果你正经历危机：在美国或加拿大，可以拨打或短信 988；在台湾可拨打 1925；在香港可致电 2389 2222 或 2896 0000；紧急情况请拨打当地紧急电话。

政策变更

如有更新，我们会修改本页并更新生效日期。

Ten Years From Now – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Ten Years From Now (十年后的我) helps you picture yourself ten years ahead and plan backward to today. This policy explains what information we process, why, and the choices you have.

No account required

The app does not require sign-up or login and does not collect your name, email address, phone number or other identity information.

Content sent to AI

When you generate a portrait or a backward plan, or chat with your future self, the descriptions you enter (such as who you want to become and where you are today) and your chat messages are forwarded through our server (hosted on Cloudflare Workers) to Anthropic's Claude API to generate content. Our server only relays them and does not store or log this content. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms and privacy policy; it may keep it for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring, and by default does not use it to train models.

Please do not enter sensitive information such as ID numbers, bank card numbers or passwords.

Data on your device and in iCloud

Your portraits, plans, daily tasks, evening reflections, letters to the future and chat history are stored on your device and synced through Apple's iCloud key-value storage to your own iCloud account, so they can be restored on a new phone. We cannot read your iCloud data. You can permanently delete this data from your device and iCloud at any time in Me → Delete All Data.

Anonymous device ID and rate limiting

To prevent abuse and control costs, the app creates a random anonymous device ID that is sent with each request so the server can count daily AI usage. This count is kept on the server for at most 2 days. The server also briefly uses your IP address for short-term rate limiting and does not store it separately. The anonymous device ID and the number of free chats used are stored in the device keychain and remain after the app is deleted, to prevent free chats from being claimed repeatedly; they contain no personal information.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details; the app only learns from Apple whether a subscription is active.

Notifications

Evening reflection reminders and letter reminders are scheduled locally on your device and do not pass through our server. You can turn them off in the app or in system Settings.

What we don't do

The app does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell any data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Not professional advice

AI-generated portraits, plans and replies are for reference only and are not professional career, medical, legal, financial or mental health advice. If you are in crisis: in the US or Canada, call or text 988; in Taiwan, call 1925; in Hong Kong, call 2389 2222 or 2896 0000; in an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change this page and its effective date.
