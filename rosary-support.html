高級版訂閱 · Subscription

玫瑰經、慈悲串經和基本經文永遠免費。高級版是可選的自動續訂訂閱（按月或按年），包括：六個九日敬禮（含聖誕九日敬禮）、經文朗讀、不限數量的祈禱意向和完整經文集。

付款在確認購買時從你的 Apple 帳號扣除。除非在當前訂閱期結束前至少 24 小時關閉自動續訂，訂閱會自動續訂。你可以在 App Store 帳號設定中管理或取消訂閱；在新裝置上可以在 App 的「設定 → 恢復購買」恢復。

The Rosary, the Divine Mercy Chaplet and the basic prayers are always free. Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) that adds six novenas (including a Christmas Novena), read-aloud, unlimited prayer intentions and the full prayer collection. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings; on a new device, use Settings → Restore Purchases in the app.

你的資料 · Your data

祈禱紀錄、意向和九日敬禮進度只保存在你的裝置上，不會上傳。你可以隨時在「設定 → 清除全部資料」刪除。

Your prayer log, intentions and novena progress stay on your device and are never uploaded. Delete them any time with Settings → Erase All Data.
