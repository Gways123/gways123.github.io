Privacy Policy

Quiet Temple · Last updated November 19, 2026

Summary

Quiet Temple does not collect, store on servers, sell or share any personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

Your practice history (dates and counts of bows, beads, wooden fish strikes, incense, sits), the lantern intentions you write, and your sound settings are saved locally on your device using standard iOS storage. They are not transmitted to us or to any third party. Deleting the app, or using Erase practice history in Settings, removes them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. Apple’s handling of your data is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Quiet Temple contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Quiet Temple does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
