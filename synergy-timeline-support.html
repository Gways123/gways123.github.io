Synergy Timeline – Support · 帮助与支持

Synergy Timeline is a shared relationship journal for two people. Record the moments you share, see them on one timeline, and look at a gentle day-by-day rhythm view.

Synergy Timeline 是两个人共用的关系日记：记录一起经历的时刻，放在同一条时间线上，并查看每日的双人节律。

Subscription · 订阅

Synergy Timeline Premium is an optional auto-renewable monthly subscription. It unlocks unlimited moments (free: 5), photos on moments, the 90-day rhythm view (free: 30 days) and PDF keepsake export. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. The subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings → Restore Purchases in the app.

高级版为可选的按月自动续订订阅：不限数量的时刻（免费版 5 个）、为时刻添加照片、90 天节律（免费版 30 天）以及导出 PDF 纪念册。可在 App Store 账户设置中管理或取消订阅；在新设备上可通过 App 内"设置 → 恢复购买"恢复。

Your data · 你的数据

Everything you enter — names, dates, moments, notes and photos — is stored only on your device. You can erase it at any time in Settings. 所有内容只保存在你的设备上，可随时在设置中删除。
