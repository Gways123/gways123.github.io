Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my practice history stored?
Only on your device. Quiet Temple has no account and does not upload your practice, intentions or lanterns.

How do I restore Premium on a new device?
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device. See Apple’s guide.

How do I request a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Subscription terms
Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage and cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

Is Quiet Temple affiliated with a temple or Buddhist organization?
No. It is an independent app offering a calm space for reflection, inspired by Buddhist temple practices. It does not provide medical or psychological advice.
