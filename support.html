Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan an ingredient list?

Tap "Scan an ingredient list" on the home screen to use your camera, or tap "Pick from gallery" to select a photo. You can also paste the ingredient text directly into the text box.

What do the colors mean?

Green (Gentle): Well-tolerated by most skin types.
Yellow (Caution): May cause sensitivity in some people.
Amber (Active): Active ingredients that require careful use.

How do I restore my subscription?

Tap "Restore purchases" on the subscription screen to restore a previous purchase.

How many free scans do I get?

You get 3 free scans per day. Subscribe for unlimited scans.
