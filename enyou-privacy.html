恩雨 – 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 20 日

恩雨（Enyou）是一款中文属灵陪伴 App。本政策说明我们处理哪些信息、为什么处理，以及你可以怎样控制。

不需要账户

恩雨不要求注册或登录，不收集姓名、邮箱、电话等身份信息。

对话内容

你在对话页发送的文字，会通过我们的服务器（托管于 Cloudflare Workers）转发给 Anthropic 的 Claude API，用来生成恩雨的回复。我们的服务器只做转发，不保存、不记录对话内容。Anthropic 会按照其商业服务条款和隐私政策处理这些内容，可能会为安全和滥用监测保留一段有限的时间，默认不会用于训练模型。

请不要在对话中输入身份证号、银行卡号、密码等敏感信息。

保存在你设备上的数据

对话记录、祷告记录、灵修进度和灵修日记只保存在你的设备上，不会上传到我们的服务器。你可以随时在“设置 → 删除全部数据”中永久删除它们。

匿名安装 ID 与限流

为防止滥用、控制服务成本，App 会生成一个随机的匿名安装 ID，随请求发送给服务器，用于统计每天的消息数量。这个计数在服务器上最多保留 2 天。服务器也会临时使用你的 IP 地址做短时间的频率限制，不会单独保存。删除全部数据时，匿名 ID 会一并重置。

购买与订阅

恩雨高级版的订阅通过 Apple App Store 处理。我们不会收到你的姓名、邮箱或付款信息，只会从 Apple 得知订阅是否有效。免费版每天的对话次数在你的设备上计数。

通知

每日经文提醒是在你的设备上本地安排的通知，不经过我们的服务器。你可以在 App 设置或系统设置中关闭。

不做的事

恩雨不使用第三方统计或广告 SDK，不追踪你在其他 App 或网站上的行为，不出售任何数据。

儿童

恩雨不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的信息。

不是专业服务

恩雨是 AI 陪伴者，不是牧者、心理咨询师或医疗人员。如果你正经历危机：在美国或加拿大，可以拨打或短信 988；紧急情况请拨打当地紧急电话；其他地区请联系当地紧急服务或心理危机热线。

政策变更

如有更新，我们会修改本页并更新生效日期。

Enyou – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Enyou (恩雨) is a Chinese-language Christian spiritual companion app. This policy explains what information we process, why, and the choices you have.

No account required

Enyou does not require sign-up or login, and does not collect your name, email address, phone number or other identity information.

Chat messages

Messages you send in the Chat tab are forwarded through our server (hosted on Cloudflare Workers) to Anthropic's Claude API to generate replies. Our server only relays them and does not store or log chat content. Anthropic processes this content under its commercial terms and privacy policy. It may keep it for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring, and by default does not use it to train models.

Please do not enter sensitive information such as ID numbers, bank card numbers or passwords in chat.

Data stored on your device

Your chat history, prayer notes, devotional progress and devotional journal entries are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to our server. You can permanently delete them at any time in Settings → Delete All Data.

Anonymous install ID and rate limiting

To prevent abuse and control costs, the app creates a random anonymous install ID that is sent with each request so the server can count daily messages. This count is kept on the server for at most 2 days. The server also briefly uses your IP address for short-term rate limiting and does not store it separately. The anonymous ID is reset when you delete all data.

Purchases and subscriptions

Enyou Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details; the app only learns from Apple whether a subscription is active. The free daily chat allowance is counted on your device.

Notifications

Daily verse reminders are scheduled locally on your device and do not pass through our server. You can turn them off in the app or in system Settings.

What we don't do

Enyou does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell any data.

Children

Enyou is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Not a professional service

Enyou is an AI companion, not a pastor, counselor or medical professional. If you are in crisis: in the US or Canada, call or text 988; in an emergency, call your local emergency number; elsewhere, contact local emergency services or a crisis hotline.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change this page and its effective date.
